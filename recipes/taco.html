Taco

Description

Tacos are good.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (85/15 or 90/10 lean ratio)
  • 2 tbsp taco seasoning mix
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 8 small corn or flour tortillas
  • Shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, shredded cheddar cheese, and salsa.

Steps

  1. Brown the meat: Cook the ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat for 5 to 7 minutes until no longer pink. Drain any extra grease if needed.
  2. Season and simmer: Stir in the taco seasoning and water. Let it simmer on medium-low for 3 to 4 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  3. Warm the tortillas: Heat the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave for 20 to 30 seconds until soft and flexible.
  4. Spoon the beef into each tortilla and add your choice of cheese, lettuce, and salsa.
