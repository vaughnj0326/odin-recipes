Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is good.

Ingredients

  • Ground Beef(1lb)
  • Marinara Sauce(24oz)
  • Lasagna noodles(12-15 dry noodles)
  • Ricotta cheese(16oz)
  • Mozzarella cheese(12oz shredded)
  • Seasonings(1 diced yellow onion, 2 minced garlic cloves, salt, and black pepper)

Steps

  1. Prep the oven and noodles: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Boil the lasagna noodles in salted water until just al dente, then drain.
  2. Make the meat sauce: Heat a skillet over medium heat, add the onion and ground beef, and cook until browned. Drain the excess fat, stir in the garlic, salt, pepper, and marinara sauce, and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Mix the cheese filling: In a bowl, stir together the ricotta cheese, egg, half of the parmesan, and a pinch of salt.
  4. Assemble the layers: Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Add a layer of noodles, followed by part of the ricotta mixture, a sprinkle of mozzarella, and more meat sauce. Repeat the layers.
  5. Top and bake: Finish the top layer with noodles, remaining sauce, and a heavy blanket of mozzarella and parmesan. Cover with foil and bake for 45 minutes; uncover and bake for 5 to 10 more minutes until golden and bubbly.
  6. Rest and serve: Let the lasagna rest for 15 to 30 minutes before slicing so the layers hold together.
