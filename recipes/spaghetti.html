Spaghetti

Description

Spaghetti is good.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Spaghetti noodles
  • 1 lb Ground beef
  • 3 cloves Garlic (minced)
  • 3 cups Marinara sauce
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper (to taste)
  • Parmesan cheese (for serving)

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta: Cook the spaghetti in a large pot of salted boiling water until al dente, then drain.
  2. Brown the meat: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and minced garlic, cooking until browned, then drain excess fat.
  3. Simmer the sauce: Stir the marinara sauce into the beef, season with salt and pepper, and let it simmer for 5 to 10 minutes.
  4. Combine and serve: Toss the drained spaghetti with the meat sauce, and top with grated parmesan cheese before serving.
